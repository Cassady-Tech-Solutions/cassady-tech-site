## Definition of done

A task is not done because it was discussed.

A task is done when:

- The intended outcome exists.
- The relevant person knows it is complete.
- The next action is recorded if needed.
- The result is stored where it can be found.
- Any decision or risk is documented.
- The customer was not left guessing.

## Startup rules

- Every active lead has one owner.
- Every active lead has one next action.
- Every customer promise is written.
- Every significant expense has a reason.
- Every recurring service has a defined boundary.
- Every risk has an owner.
- Every weekly meeting ends with decisions or next actions.
- Every month includes a stop decision.

## Weekly meeting agenda

Time target: 20 to 30 minutes

1. What changed since last week?
2. What is the most important outcome now?
3. What customer or lead needs attention?
4. What is waiting on approval?
5. What work should be paused?
6. What is the family's capacity this week?
7. What are the next three actions?
8. When will we review again?

## Monthly meeting agenda

Time target: 30 to 45 minutes

1. Review revenue and expenses.
2. Review pipeline and customer evidence.
3. Review time and workload.
4. Review risks and open decisions.
5. Decide stop, continue, test, and invest later.
6. Confirm next month's single primary milestone.
