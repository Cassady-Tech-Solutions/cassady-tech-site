# Website Rebuild Plan

## Scope

This plan covers the future Cassady Tech Solutions website repositioning. The Greenhorn Valley Tech site should use the same strategic framework while receiving its own residential information architecture and copy.

No rebuild or deployment is authorized by this document.

## Cassady Tech Solutions information architecture

### Home

Purpose: Establish who CTS serves, what relationship it provides, and why the visitor should begin a conversation.

Core message:

> Technology for organizations that need a dependable partner.

Sections:

1. Clear organizational technology hero
2. Who CTS helps: 5-50 employee organizations and similar small teams
3. Problems solved: downtime, account confusion, security gaps, vendor sprawl, manual work, unclear AI adoption
4. Relationship overview: technology operations, security foundations, automation, and AI enablement
5. How engagements work
6. Proof, experience, or representative outcomes
7. About Sam and the human operating model
8. Service area and remote-first delivery
9. Technology Review CTA

### Technology Operations

Purpose: Explain recurring support and operational oversight without leading with an overwhelming service catalog.

Topics:

- Microsoft 365 and identity
- Endpoint and device oversight
- Remote support
- Vendor coordination
- Documentation
- Backup and continuity oversight
- Technology planning

### Cybersecurity Foundations

Purpose: Present practical risk reduction with accurate claims.

Topics:

- Account security and MFA
- Endpoint protection
- Patch and update hygiene
- Backup and recovery readiness
- Access and device reviews
- Security-minded operations
- Clear limitations and shared responsibility

### AI and Automation

Purpose: Make the new strategic direction understandable and credible.

Topics:

- Identify useful opportunities
- Protect sensitive information
- Select appropriate tools
- Connect workflows
- Enable staff
- Document and review changes
- Keep humans accountable for important decisions

### Projects and Advisory

Purpose: Capture defined work that does not belong in recurring operations.

Potential examples:

- Microsoft 365 cleanup or setup
- New-office technology setup
- Workflow automation
- AI tool implementation
- Documentation and recovery planning
- Vendor or platform transition

### Who We Help

Purpose: Qualify visitors and make the 5-50 employee target explicit without excluding good smaller fits.

Suggested categories:

- Professional services
- Small offices
- Contractors and trades
- Nonprofits
- Community organizations
- Hybrid and distributed teams

### About

Purpose: Build trust through a human owner-led story.

Content should cover:

- Sam’s cybersecurity background
- Practical technology experience
- Local Southern Colorado connection
- Security-minded, plain-language approach
- The relationship between CTS and GHV

### Contact

Primary CTA:

> Start a Technology Review

The form should collect enough context to qualify the conversation without becoming burdensome:

- Organization name
- Contact name and preferred method
- Employee count
- Location
- Current technology pain
- Existing IT support
- Interest in operations, security, automation, AI, or a combination

## Shared visual direction

The two sites should feel like part of one family through:

- Related mountain and terrain imagery
- Shared typography rules
- Shared design discipline and spacing
- Coordinated color systems
- Subtle cross-brand references
- Consistent contact and trust patterns

They should remain distinct through:

- Different primary messages
- Different customer examples
- Different CTA language
- Different service emphasis
- Different imagery and emotional tone

## SEO and discoverability priorities

The future CTS site should target plain-language search intent around:

- Managed IT support for small businesses
- Small business technology support
- Microsoft 365 support
- Cybersecurity basics for small businesses
- AI integration for small businesses
- Business automation consulting
- Technology support in Southern Colorado

Search optimization must support clear customer understanding rather than produce pages written only for keywords.

## Rebuild sequence

1. Confirm the business model and initial offer boundaries.
2. Confirm the ICP and qualification signals.
3. Confirm the relationship and CTA.
4. Finalize sitemap and page-level content briefs.
5. Finalize visual system and shared brand rules.
6. Draft and review copy.
7. Build the CTS site in a safe branch or separate preview.
8. Test forms, accessibility, mobile layout, metadata, analytics, and deployment behavior.
9. Review against this plan and the live site.
10. Obtain explicit authorization before production deployment.
