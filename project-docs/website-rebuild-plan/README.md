# Cassady Tech Solutions + Greenhorn Valley Tech Website Rebuild Plan

This project folder contains the living strategy and implementation planning documents for the future repositioning and redesign of:

- Cassady Tech Solutions: technology for organizations
- Greenhorn Valley Tech: technology for people and homes

## Current status

Planning only. This documentation does not authorize rebuilding, replacing, or deploying either website.

The business model, service catalog, pricing, positioning, brand relationship, and messaging remain subject to refinement as customer discovery and operating experience improve.

- [STRATEGIC-BRIEF.md](./STRATEGIC-BRIEF.md): Current positioning, brand relationship, audience, and strategic boundaries.
- [ICP-AND-OFFER.md](./ICP-AND-OFFER.md): Cassady Tech Solutions ideal customer profile for organizations with 5-50 employees and the relationship to sell.
- [WEBSITE-REBUILD-PLAN.md](./WEBSITE-REBUILD-PLAN.md): Proposed information architecture, page goals, content priorities, and rebuild sequence.
- [DECISIONS-AND-OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](./DECISIONS-AND-OPEN-QUESTIONS.md): Confirmed decisions, assumptions, risks, and unresolved choices.

## Guiding principle

The websites should make the two brands feel related without making them feel interchangeable:

- Greenhorn Valley Tech serves households and individuals.
- Cassady Tech Solutions serves organizations and their technology operations.
- Both brands express practical, human-centered technology support.
