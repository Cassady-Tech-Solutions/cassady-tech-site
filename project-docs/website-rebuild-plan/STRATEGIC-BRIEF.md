# Strategic Brief

## Mission

Build two clear, trustworthy, human-centered technology brands that help Southern Colorado customers use technology with less confusion, risk, and disruption.

## Brand architecture

### Greenhorn Valley Tech

North star: **Technology for people and homes**

Primary audience:

- Households
- Individuals
- Older adults and less technical customers
- Home-based workers
- Families needing practical help with devices, Wi-Fi, accounts, smart-home technology, and digital safety

Primary promise:

> Make technology at home work the way it should, with a real person available when it does not.

### Cassady Tech Solutions

North star: **Technology for organizations**

Primary audience:

- Small organizations with approximately 5-50 employees
- Owner-led businesses
- Professional services firms
- Nonprofits and community organizations
- Local offices with limited internal IT capacity
- Organizations adopting Microsoft 365, automation, and AI without a dedicated technology strategist

Primary promise:

> Give small organizations a dependable technology partner who improves operations, reduces avoidable risk, and helps them adopt useful technology responsibly.

## Shared philosophy

Both brands should communicate:

- Practical help over unnecessary complexity
- Human support over anonymous ticket queues
- Security and resilience as part of good technology
- Clear explanations and honest boundaries
- Local understanding with remote-first efficiency
- Technology that serves people, not the other way around

## Relationship between the brands

Greenhorn Valley Tech is the residential and household-facing brand.

Cassady Tech Solutions is the organizational and business-facing brand.

They may share visual language, values, ownership, and technical capability, but each site must answer a different customer question:

- GHV: “Can you help me make technology work in my home?”
- CTS: “Can you help my organization operate and improve through technology?”

## Strategic repositioning

Cassady Tech Solutions is evolving from a traditional small-business MSP presentation toward a broader technology partnership model combining:

- Managed technology operations
- Practical cybersecurity
- Microsoft 365 and identity support
- Automation and workflow improvement
- AI readiness, integration, governance, and enablement
- Documentation and technology decision support

The site should not prematurely claim enterprise-grade security, formal compliance consulting, guaranteed protection, or autonomous AI transformation. Claims must remain proportional to demonstrated capability and the selected engagement.

## Non-negotiable planning boundary

This brief is a living model. It guides discovery and content planning. It is not authorization to rebuild or deploy either website.
