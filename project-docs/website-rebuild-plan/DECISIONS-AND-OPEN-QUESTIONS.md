# Decisions and Open Questions

## Confirmed direction

- CTS serves organizations.
- GHV serves people and homes.
- CTS's initial ICP target is approximately 5-50 employees.
- CTS should sell an ongoing technology partnership, not only break-fix support.
- AI integration, automation, and governance are part of CTS's direction.
- Practical cybersecurity remains an important foundation.
- The two brands should feel related but remain clearly distinct.
- The rebuild plan is strategic documentation only at this stage.
- No website rebuild or production deployment is authorized yet.

## Current assumptions

- A local, owner-led, remote-first model is a meaningful differentiator.
- The strongest customers will have recurring technology friction and decision needs.
- A Technology Review is a better initial CTA than a generic contact form.
- The website should explain the relationship before listing every technical service.
- A broad small-organization focus is preferable to prematurely committing to one vertical.

## Questions to resolve before implementation

1. What recurring service tiers, if any, will CTS actually sell at launch?
2. Which AI and automation services can Sam deliver confidently today?
3. What is included in a Technology Review, and what is its price or sales process?
4. Which tools will be supported, recommended, or excluded?
5. What response-time expectations can CTS reliably meet?
6. Will CTS publicly publish pricing, starting prices, or no pricing?
7. What proof can be used on the site: testimonials, case studies, experience, or representative outcomes?
8. What geographic language should be used: Southern Colorado, specific towns, or remote-first statewide service?
9. How should residential referrals move between GHV and CTS?
10. What legal, privacy, and disclaimer language is needed for AI, cybersecurity, and regulated industries?

## Change control

When a strategic decision changes, update this file and the affected plan documents together. Do not treat older website copy, pricing, or service documents as authoritative without checking this plan.
